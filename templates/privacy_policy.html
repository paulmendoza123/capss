🔒 Data Privacy Statement
SPARK Exam Monitoring System

This statement explains what SPARK monitors during an online exam, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over that data. It applies to all students, teachers, and administrators using the system.

1. What is monitored during an exam

While an exam session is ongoing, SPARK may record:

  • Tab switches, window/app focus loss, and browser minimizing
  • Exiting fullscreen mode, if the teacher requires fullscreen for that exam
  • Screenshot or screen-recording attempts detected by the browser
  • Connection activity (online/offline heartbeat), so teachers know if you disconnected
  • Timestamps of when you started, answered questions, and submitted

SPARK does not access your webcam, microphone, files outside the browser tab, or any other application on your device.

2. Why this data is collected

This information exists solely to protect academic integrity — helping teachers identify possible cheating during a timed, proctored exam — and to give you a fair, accurate record if your session is ever disputed (e.g., a disconnect during submission).

3. Who can see it

Monitoring logs for an exam are visible only to the teacher who owns that exam/class and to system administrators. Other students never see your monitoring activity.

4. How long it is kept

Monitoring logs are retained for as long as the related exam record exists in the system, so results and disputes can be reviewed for that term. Logs are permanently deleted when a teacher or admin deletes the associated exam.

5. Your consent

Before any monitoring begins, you will be shown a consent screen at the start of each exam. Monitoring does not start until you explicitly agree. If you do not agree, you will not be able to begin that exam, since fair proctoring requires it to be active for all students.

6. Questions or concerns

If you have questions about how your data is used, please raise them with your teacher or the system administrator before starting an exam.

Last updated: this statement is reviewed each academic term.
